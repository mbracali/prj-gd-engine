## Guitar Hero 2 (gd02)

Overall very good. Local renderer had initial problems, fixed by switching to Vulkan.

- **01 Characters transparency** (most glaring): characters look partly
  transparent, every moment, all modes and venues, after playing with HDR,
  restarts, backend and settings changes and changing characters. Johnny
  Napalm fine, Pandora the worst. Not reproduced on Linux with the same
  build. Fix: characters never transparent. Investigate.
- **02 Micro stutters** on the character select screen when moving between
  characters, every time; seems Windows only.
- **03 Dark menus**: after a song (You Rock, the stats screen with the
  character picture, the save-record screen) the last two screens are
  sometimes darker than they should be. Maybe related to Enhanced settings;
  some Enhanced options also make gameplay too dark. Menus need consistent
  lighting.
- **04 Gauges and UI kept on screen** after a song ends in multiplayer (check
  single player too). They must disappear when the song ends.
- **05 Label overlap in the setlist**: long song names overlap the stars and
  score (quick play and career, single and multi). No overlap and no line
  jumps: do what the original does.
