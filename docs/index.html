A labor of love

Guitar Dream Engine rebuilds the engines of the big Guitar Hero and Rock Band games from scratch, so the games you own can be played on today's computers, the Steam Deck and Android, with your guitar, a gamepad or the keyboard.

One launcher, every game

The Guitar Dream Universal Launcher lists every game, finds your disc images and plays each game in the same window.

  • Use your original ISO files to run the original games.
  • Explore the models of your discs: characters, guitars, venues.
  • Windows, Linux, Steam Deck, macOS and Android.
  • Guitar, gamepad, keyboard or mouse.
  • Mod support in development!
